# Marketing keyword pillars

<!-- mol:note:topic:marketing-keyword-pillars -->

Use this frame before drafting or revising any MolCrafts marketing copy. It is
the canonical identity, differentiation, and construction frame and supersedes
conflicting vocabulary or positioning in older harness material.
`.agents/product-marketing.md` continues to govern verified facts, audiences,
brand voice, and homepage content boundaries.

| Message job | Canonical English keywords |
| --- | --- |
| What we are | Molecular · AI-assisted · Infrastructure · Ecosystem |
| What makes it different | Modern · Open-source |
| How it is built | Modular · Extensible · Integrated · Interoperable |

## Rule

- Build the message around these three questions in this order: what MolCrafts
  is, what makes it different, and how it is built.
- Treat the keywords as semantic anchors, not a checklist to repeat mechanically
  in every sentence. Prefer specific, natural marketing language over keyword
  stuffing.
- Preserve these terms and their meaning in English. Chinese and Swedish copy
  should express the same concepts idiomatically while keeping `MolCrafts`
  untranslated.
- On the homepage, describe “how it is built” at the company-value level. Do not
  turn Modular, Extensible, Integrated, or Interoperable into APIs, dependencies,
  architecture diagrams, installation detail, or developer documentation.
- Any resulting user-facing wording still requires the operator's prior approval
  under the copy-approval law before implementation.
